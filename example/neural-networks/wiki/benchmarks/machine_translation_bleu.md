# Machine Translation BLEU

BLEU is the primary translation-quality metric. Transformer Big achieves 28.4 on WMT14 English–German and 41.0 on English–French. These scores are benchmark-specific and do not directly establish performance in other domains [[vaswani_attention_2017]].
