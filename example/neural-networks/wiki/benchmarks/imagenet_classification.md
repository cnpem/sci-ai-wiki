# ImageNet Classification

The paper reports top-1 and top-5 error. ResNet-152 reaches 19.38% top-1 and 4.49% top-5 validation error in the single-model comparison; the reported ensemble achieves 3.57% top-5 test error [[he_deep_residual_2016]].
