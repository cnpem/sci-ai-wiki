# Handwritten Digit Recognition Case Studies

The paper compares learning methods on standard handwritten digit-recognition settings and uses them to motivate CNNs and LeNet-5. Exact historical protocols should be preserved when reproducing the results; they should not be treated as modern benchmark equivalents [[lecun_gradient_based_1998]].
