# Trainable Feature Extraction

Trainable feature extraction learns representations jointly with the task objective instead of fixing a hand-designed front end. [[lecun_gradient_based_1998]] presents CNNs and document-recognition systems as evidence that this can simplify recognition-system design, while retaining task-specific architectural structure.
