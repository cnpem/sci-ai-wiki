# Residual versus Plain Network Depth

Matched plain and residual CNNs compare shortcut connections while controlling depth, width, parameter count, and approximate computation. Plain-34 has higher training error than plain-18, while ResNet-34 improves over ResNet-18 and has much lower error than plain-34. This supports an optimization advantage for residual parameterization, not a depth-only explanation [[he_deep_residual_2016]].
