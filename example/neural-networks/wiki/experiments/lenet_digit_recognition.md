# LeNet Handwritten Recognition Experiment

- Task: handwritten digit/character recognition.
- Model components: convolutional feature extraction, shared filters, subsampling, nonlinear layers, and trainable classifier.
- Training: gradient-based backpropagation.
- Metrics: recognition error/accuracy as reported in the paper’s historical comparisons.
- Interpretation: evidence that jointly learned CNN features can outperform hand-designed feature pipelines in the reported setting; not directly comparable to modern datasets or compute budgets.
