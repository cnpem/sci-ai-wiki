# Reported ResNet Results

Key reported results include ImageNet validation top-1 errors of 25.03% for ResNet-34, 20.74% for ResNet-50, 19.87% for ResNet-101, and 19.38% for ResNet-152. The paper also reports gains on PASCAL VOC and MS COCO detection when replacing VGG-16 features with ResNet-101. These are results under the paper’s training and evaluation pipeline, not isolated estimates of depth alone [[he_deep_residual_2016]].
